Trenning is designed to completely transform the way learning happens. By integrating advanced artificial intelligence, the platform not only streamlines the learning process but also automates material creation, making it easier than ever for educators to design high-quality, personalized content.

Initially, we used Inform LMS, but it came with numerous bugs and didn’t fully meet our needs. Although we had explored various LMS options, none provided the complete solution we were looking for. After auditing Inform, we identified several pain points, including dark UX elements and a confusing history feature. To improve it, we integrated additional features like collaboration tools, course management, and more, ensuring it was both optimized and kept up-to-date for modern users.

At that time, LMS solutions were mostly designed for schools, so we set out to create one exclusively for companies.

The Challenge

The challenge we faced at the time was the lack of strong LMS competitors. Some platforms had decent features, but the visuals were underwhelming, and many quizzes were unengaging and too basic. This also applied to content creation, which was quite limited back then.

The Solution

We aimed to introduce improved versions of popular features already available in the market, enhancing their functionality and usability. Alongside these upgrades, we carefully added new features that we felt were essential and relevant to meet today’s evolving demands, ensuring the platform stays current and valuable to users.
